# Carbide List for Claude

Carbide List is a punch-list app for home builders, their crews, and homeowners. This plugin connects Claude to your Carbide List account and teaches it how builders and crews use it.

## Who it is for

- **Builders** connect by signing in to their Carbide List account. Claude can show what is open, ready for review, and blocked across every house, turn walkthrough notes into punch-list items after you confirm them, sort items by trade, and record what an item is waiting on. You choose these permissions on the consent screen.
- **Foremen and crew leads** connect with the Crew link their Builder sent them. Claude sees only the houses the Builder shared with that crew, in English or Spanish. It can list the crew's work, track what an item is waiting on, suggest where to buy parts, add notes, and mark work ready for the Builder to review.

## Setup

1. Install the plugin, then open its Connectors tab and connect Carbide List.
2. Builders: choose **Sign in to continue**. Crews: paste your Crew link under **On a crew?**
3. Review the permissions and approve.

## What Claude can never do

Claude cannot verify or close work, delete anything, manage photos, invite people, or see a house you do not have access to. Builders can see and turn off crew connections under Crews in Carbide List. Replacing a Crew link also turns off every connection made with it.
